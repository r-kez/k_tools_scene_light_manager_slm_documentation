Parametric Studio Rigs & Presets

Scene Light Manager (SLM) features pre-calibrated, interactive studio lighting rigs. Spawning a rig automatically builds collection hierarchies, empty control handles, tracking targets, and constraints to let you manipulate professional setups instantly.

── 1. Spawning a Lighting Rig ──

Under the LIGHTS tab of the SLM sidebar, click the Presets dropdown. Selecting any rig preset automatically triggers the following pipeline:

  • Generates a dedicated collection named after the rig category.
  • Spawns a tracking Target empty at the 3D Cursor.
  • Applies Track To constraints to the spawned lights, forcing them to look at the target.
  • Spawns empty controller handles for global offsets, rotation, and distance parameters.

── 2. Built-in Rig Presets ──

3-Point Light Rig (Classic Setup) The gold standard for portraiture and product rendering. Spawns a Key light (primary exposure), a soft Fill light (shadow balance), and a Rim/Back light (separates subject silhouettes from dark backgrounds).
Studio Duo A clean, mirror-like dual-light configuration flanking both sides of the subject. Highly suited for minimalist layouts, vehicles, and clean product design presentations.
Warm-Cool Split Sets up opposing temperature light colors (amber/orange vs. cool blue) to create dramatic color contrasts. Fits stylized cinematic concepts or sci-fi environments.
Dramatic Rim Arranges intense directional lights behind and adjacent to the subject, casting hard silhouettes and tracing outline shapes, leaving the camera-facing surfaces dark.
Butterfly (Beauty Setup) Spawns the main emitter directly above and slightly in front of the camera path. Minimizes surface wrinkles and textures, classic in high-fashion modeling renders.
Double Sun Mimics a binary star environment, generating stylized double directional highlights and shadow structures. Ideal for sci-fi desert landscapes and artistic concepts.

── 3. Controlling Rigs (Parametric Sliders) ──

Selecting a rig's collection or empty controller handle in the SLM list opens the Rig Properties sidebar panel, containing specialized sliders:

  • Global Scale & Distance: Adjusts the distance of all emitters from the target, scaling the entire rig radius symmetrically.
  • Base Energy & Contrast: Base Energy acts as a global exposure multiplier for the entire rig. Contrast scales the energy ratio between Key and Fill lights to control shadow hardness.
  • Height & Tracking Height: Height raises or lowers the emitters vertically. Tracking Height moves the look-at target empty up or down to concentrate lights on specific features.
