Interactive Viewport Tools

Scene Light Manager (SLM) features high-performance viewport tools that allow you to adjust lights, rotate HDRI skies, and isolate illumination sources directly in the 3D viewport under your mouse cursor, avoiding N-Panel slider hunting.

── 1. Interactive HDRI & Sky Rotation ──

Instead of editing shader mapping nodes or diving into World tabs to rotate environment maps, SLM provides real-time Z-axis rotation shortcuts directly in the viewport.

Keyboard & Mouse Controls

  • Ctrl + Alt + RMB (Drag)
    Trigger Rotation Hold the keys and drag the mouse horizontally in the viewport to rotate the active sky coordinate system.
  • Left Click or Enter
    Confirm Action Saves the current rotation angle.
  • Escape or Right Click
    Cancel & Reset Discards changes and resets coordinates to their initial values.

Smart Context Detection

The rotation operator dynamically evaluates the active scene setup:

Nishita Sky Active Rotates the native Sun Angle coordinate.
Custom HDRI mapping Drives rotation of Z-axis inputs in the Mapping Node.
Material Preview Mode Drives native viewport studio HDRI rotation values.

── 2. Viewport Gizmo Buttons ──

Selecting any light source in the scene reveals a row of floating, transparent viewport button overlays next to the object pivot.

Type Selector (Lightbulb) Cycles the light source type (Area, Point, Spot, Sun).
Shape Cycles (Square/Circle) Toggles Area light geometries (Square, Disk, Rectangle, Ellipse).
Size Controller (Arrows) Click and drag to scale physical dimensions or radius.
Beam/Spread (Concentric Circles) Click and drag to scale Area spread angles or Spot blending limits.

[NOTE] Customizing Gizmos: You can toggle individual button visibility, change sizes, spaces, offsets, and transparency settings under the Addon Preferences.

── 3. Quick Popup Menu (Y Key) ──

Pressing Y in the 3D viewport spawns a complete parameters editor card directly at your mouse coordinates:

  • SETTINGS Tab Offers quick adjustments for color temperatures, exposures, intensities, rig controls, constraints, and visibility toggles.
  • NODES Tab Enables nodes, binds Gobo structures, loads projection maps, and displays shader preview rendering thumbnails.
  • WORLD Tab Swaps background HDRI maps, manages Nishita Sky presets, and drives Z-rotations.

── 4. Smart Light Isolation (Solo Mode) ──

Mutes all other lights in the scene instantly to isolate and tweak the active light source's shadows and color bounces.

  • Activation: Click the Star/Solo button on the sidebar list or click the Star icon in the Viewport Gizmo.
  • State Preservation: All original visibility statuses are cached. Toggling solo mode off returns the scene to its exact prior visibility layout.
  • Mute HDRI: Configurable preference to automatically black out world backgrounds while soloing lights to analyze light leaks.

── 5. Interactive Light Placement (Pointer) ──

Project lights precisely onto your geometry. Selecting a light and running pointer placement will cause the light source to snap and align along the normals of surfaces under your cursor, pointing directly at the target highlight area. Useful for creating cinematic hair highlights, precise rim accents, and character eye specular spots.
